Ajeenkya DY Patil University

ADYPU is a modern university located in Pune, Maharashtra. It offers courses in areas like design, engineering, management, and law. The university focuses on creativity, innovation, and practical learning. It provides students with opportunities to explore their interests and develop new skills. Overall, ADYPU has a modern and student-friendly learning environment.
